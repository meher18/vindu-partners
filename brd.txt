

Business Requirements
Vindu — Master Business Requirements Document (BRD v2)
Restructured for development. This document is organized by Build Phase — each phase can be developed, tested, and shipped independently. All previously finalized decisions are preserved.

⚠️ Loophole & Edge Case Analysis (Read First)
Before dividing into build phases, here are all identified gaps and edge cases with resolutions:

#	Loophole / Edge Case	Resolution
1	No address at browse time	Customer sees generic "12–2 PM" window + prompt to add address. ETA preview unlocks only after address is saved.
2	Two lunch subscriptions simultaneously	✅ Allowed from different vendors. Each is an independent delivery with its own ETA card. Same vendor, same slot = blocked.
3	Vendor changes slot timing mid-subscription	Existing subscribers are notified. Change takes effect from the next subscription cycle only, not current.
4	Driver picks up from multiple kitchens in same slot	Each kitchen generates its own QR code. Driver scans kitchen-by-kitchen. Route is ordered: kitchen A pickup → deliver A customers → kitchen B pickup → deliver B customers.
5	QR code window expiry	Pickup QR is valid for 2 hours from generation. If not scanned within 2 hours, it expires and triggers vendor + driver alert.
6	OTP expiry for customer delivery	Delivery OTP is valid for 30 minutes after driver marks "arrived". If expired, driver can request a new one from the app.
7	Vendor SO late that driver's window overlaps another customer	Dynamic driver reassignment. If vendor marks ready 60+ min late AND the current driver's updated deadline falls outside the slot window: (1) System immediately checks for available backup drivers in the zone. (2) If backup found → reassign affected deliveries to backup driver with recalculated SLA. Vendor is still penalized for their delay. (3) If no backup → delivery marked "Failed — Vendor Fault". Customer gets full wallet refund. Vendor absorbs 50% penalty.
8	New subscriber joins mid-month	They are added to the batch job starting the next day. Route is recalculated at midnight to include them.
9	Subscription expires on delivery day	System checks: if today is the last day of the plan, it still delivers. Auto-renewal (if enabled) triggers at midnight before the delivery, not after.
10	Wallet has MORE credits than plan cost	Excess stays in wallet. Customer pays ₹0 and wallet balance reduces by the plan cost. No "change" is issued outside wallet.
11	Premium bought mid-subscription	Flexi Skip applies immediately to ALL active subscriptions from the moment of purchase. No waiting for next cycle.
12	Custom subscriptions in Milk Run route	Custom slot deliveries are batched separately from standard slots. They run as a mini Milk Run within their own time window.
13	Driver doesn't show up for shift	Escalation ladder: (T-30 min) App pings driver if not online → admin alerted. (T-0) Admin calls driver. (+15 min) Admin searches backup driver pool. (+30 min) If no driver found → vendor is asked "Can you self-deliver today?" → customers notified "Delivery delayed — would you prefer takeaway?" (refund delivery fee to wallet). If neither vendor nor customer can resolve → full wallet refund to customer. Driver is penalized / removed from platform after repeated no-shows.
14	Vendor reduces capacity below current subscribers	Existing subscribers are grandfathered. Capacity reduction only affects new subscriptions. Vendor is warned if they attempt to go below active subscriber count.
15	Customer address on zone border	Customer's pin code determines their zone. If a pin code spans two zones, the system assigns to the nearest kitchen's zone.
16	Skip cutoff at exactly 8:00 PM	Server-side enforcement only. If the request arrives at the server at 20:00:01, it is rejected. Client UI disables the toggle at 8:00 PM as a UX hint only.
17	Rating after failed delivery	If delivery is marked "Failed/Wasted", the rating prompt does NOT appear. Customer cannot rate what they didn't receive.
18	Vendor goes offline mid-subscription	Admin suspends vendor. All affected active subscriptions are paused. Customers receive wallet credit for remaining days. Prorated vendor fee is not paid for undelivered days.
19	Weather / force majeure	No-fault resolution — everyone is partially protected. Customer: full wallet credit (no money lost). Vendor: paid their Vendor Fee (they prepared the food and showed up). Driver: paid base shift rate (they showed up/attempted). Vindu absorbs the Delivery Fee as operational cost. No SLA penalty for any party. Admin manually flags the delivery as "Force Majeure" with evidence (weather alert, etc.).
20	Pricing transparency	Customer sees the total lump sum at checkout. The vendor fee vs delivery fee split is an internal ledger detail and is NOT shown to customers by default. Optional "price breakdown" can be added post-MVP.
21	Address outside vendor's delivery zone	Customer cannot subscribe to a vendor whose delivery zone doesn't include their pin code. A "Not delivering to your area" state is shown on the subscription card.
22	Holiday closures	Declared at subscription creation time. When vendor creates a subscription, they mark all known no-delivery dates (holidays, maintenance days) upfront. These dates are shown to the customer on the subscription detail page before subscribing — full transparency. If vendor adds a new holiday after customers have subscribed, all affected subscribers are notified and wallet-credited for that day.
23	ETA preview accuracy	Browse-time ETA is an estimate. A disclaimer always reads: "Exact time may vary ±15 min based on daily route".
24	Driver with 40+ stops and cascading delays	Driver's per-stop delivery deadline is calculated independently. Late arrival at stop 1 doesn't automatically fail stop 2's SLA — each stop is evaluated on its own scanned QR timestamp.
Build Phases
The entire product is divided into 9 build phases in strict dependency order. Each phase produces a shippable feature.

PHASE 1 — Foundation: Database Schema & Auth
Goal: Everything built on top depends on this. Build it once, build it right.

1.1 Database Tables Required
Table	Key Fields
profiles	id, role (customer/vendor/driver), full_name, phone, delivery_address, lat, lng
kitchens	id, vendor_id, name, address, lat, lng, zone_id, fssai_number, status
delivery_zones	id, name, pin_codes[ ], polygon (for map)
subscriptions	id, kitchen_id, diet_type, duration_type, slot_name, slot_target_time, delivery_type, price_per_day, vendor_fee, delivery_fee, capacity, status
customer_subscriptions	id, customer_id, subscription_id, start_date, end_date, status, premium_unlocked
menus	id, kitchen_id, slot_id, effective_date, status (pending/active), items[ ]
skips	id, customer_subscription_id, date, credited_amount
deliveries	id, customer_subscription_id, date, status, vendor_ready_at, qr_scanned_at, delivered_at, driver_id, proof_photo_url, otp_code
wallets	id, customer_id, balance
wallet_transactions	id, wallet_id, amount, type, description, delivery_id
vendor_ledger	id, kitchen_id, delivery_id, gross_amount, penalty_amount, net_amount, status
driver_ledger	id, driver_id, delivery_id, gross_amount, penalty_amount, net_amount, status
ratings	id, delivery_id, customer_id, kitchen_id, stars, review_text, photo_url, created_at
premium_purchases	id, customer_id, purchased_at, payment_id
1.2 RLS Policies (Row Level Security)
Customers: read/write own profile, subscriptions, deliveries, wallet, skips
Vendors: read/write own kitchen, menus, subscriptions, ledger; read own delivery counts
Drivers: read assigned deliveries; write delivery status, qr_scanned_at, delivered_at
Public: read active kitchens, active subscriptions (for browse)
1.3 Auth
Email + password via Supabase Auth
On signup: create profiles row + wallets row (auto, via DB trigger)
Role assignment: default = customer; vendor/driver roles set by admin approval
PHASE 2 — Vendor Onboarding & Kitchen Setup
Goal: Enable vendors to onboard, set up their kitchen, and create subscriptions.

Features
Vendor registration form (kitchen name, address, FSSAI number, bank details, zone selection)
Admin approval flow (simple status toggle in admin panel)
Kitchen profile page (name, photo, description, cuisine tags, operating hours)
Delivery zone assignment (vendor selects zones they deliver to — must be in their geographic area)
Slot configuration:
Standard slots: Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner (vendor sets the target delivery time, e.g., "Lunch by 1:00 PM")
Custom slots: vendor defines name + target time
Subscription creation (vendor creates plans with: diet type, duration type, price, capacity, delivery type)
Vendor capacity management (set max subscribers per subscription)
Key Business Rules
A vendor cannot serve a zone if their kitchen is more than X km from the zone boundary (TBD)
Vendor-set target delivery times must be within reasonable bounds per slot:
Breakfast: 7:00 AM – 10:00 AM
Lunch: 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM
Dinner: 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM
PHASE 3 — Menu Management
Goal: Enable vendors to publish rotating menus with the 2-hour delay system.

Features
Menu editor: add items per slot per date (name, description, photo, tags)
Effective date selector with system-enforced 2-hour delay for same-day new menus
Status system: pending → active (auto-transition via Supabase scheduled function)
Menu preview for vendor before going live
Edit existing menu items: NO delay (immediate, for fixing typos/photos)
Banner system: "This menu is available from [date/time]"
Business Rules
2-hour delay applies to new menu creation only, not edits
Vendor is shown a warning prompt before saving a same-day effective menu
Menus can be scheduled up to 7 days in advance
PHASE 4 — Customer Discovery & Subscription Purchase
Goal: Customers can browse, compare, and subscribe to meal plans.

Features
Address-based onboarding gate: Customer must save delivery address to unlock personalized ETAs
Browse/discovery screen with all filters:
Diet type: Veg / Non-Veg / Fruits / Customized
Duration: Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Custom
Delivery type: Home Delivery / Take Away
Meal slot: Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Custom
Sort: Top Rated / Earliest Delivery / Price Low to High / Newest
Keyword search
Subscription card shows:
Kitchen name, rating, distance, price/day
Est. delivery to you: ~12:45 PM (calculated from vendor's Mark-Ready time + Google Maps distance to saved address)
⚠️ Exact time may vary ±15 min
"Not delivering to your area" if customer's zone isn't served
Subscription detail page (menu preview, kitchen profile, ratings)
Favourites (save/unsave subscriptions)
Subscription checkout flow:
Select plan duration (3-day trial / daily bundle / weekly / monthly)
Select quantity (e.g., 1 to 4 portions for family orders)
Select start date
Wallet credits auto-applied
Payment via Razorpay
On success: customer_subscriptions row created
ETA Calculation at Browse Time

Est. ETA = Vendor Mark-Ready time
           + QR pickup window (30 min)
           + Google Maps travel time (vendor kitchen → customer address)
PHASE 5 — Customer Calendar & Wallet (Skip/Pause System)
Goal: Enable customers to manage their deliveries and track credits.

Features
Calendar view: visual monthly grid showing each delivery day
🟢 Scheduled | 🟡 Skipped (Premium) | 🔴 Failed | ⚫ No delivery
Skip toggle: tap any future date to skip (Premium users only; toggle disabled after 8:00 PM server-side)
8 PM cutoff: server validates skip request timestamp before crediting wallet
Wallet screen:
Main Wallet balance (spendable)
Subscription spend ledger per subscription (read-only transaction history)
Transaction types: -₹10 (Meal delivered), +₹10 (Delivery failed), +₹10 (Skipped — Premium)
Premium purchase: one-time payment to unlock Flexi Skip for all subscriptions
Wallet cross-vendor: credits apply to any vendor's subscription renewal
Flexi Skip Limits
Plan	Max Skips
Daily (30-day)	5 days
Weekly	1 week
Monthly	Full month pause (delays next renewal)
Custom	No skips
PHASE 6 — Midnight Batch Job & Route Optimization
Goal: Automate daily delivery generation, SLA calculation, and driver route assignment.

Supabase Edge Function — runs at 12:00 AM daily
Step 1: Find all active customer_subscriptions where today is within start_date–end_date

Step 2: Cross-reference skips table — exclude skipped dates

Step 3: For each remaining delivery, calculate SLA deadlines:


Vendor Mark-Ready deadline = Slot target time − Max(travel times in batch) − 30 min
Driver QR Scan deadline    = Vendor Mark-Ready deadline + 30 min
Driver delivery deadline   = QR Scan time + individual travel time + 5 min buffer
Step 4: Google Maps Routes API — optimize stop order per driver per zone

Step 5: Assign deliveries to drivers (by zone)

Step 6: Create deliveries rows with all SLA timestamps pre-populated

Step 7: Send 6:00 AM push notification to customers with their personalized ETA

Step 8: Send fulfillment count to vendors (how many meals to prepare per slot)

Failure Alerting
If batch job fails: send SMS + email to admin immediately
Retry once automatically after 15 minutes
If retry fails: admin manually triggers re-run
PHASE 7 — Delivery Execution (Vendor + Driver App)
Goal: Execute the actual day's deliveries with QR verification and SLA tracking.

Vendor App Features
Today's fulfillment count (per slot, updates after 8 PM skip cutoff)
"Mark Shipment Ready" button → generates pickup QR code
Emergency Pause button: Pauses next day's deliveries. Note: Using this even once negatively impacts the vendor's ranking/Top Kitchen status.
QR code display screen (full-screen, high contrast for easy scanning)
SLA countdown timer visible ("Ready deadline: 12:00 PM — 47 min remaining")
Real-time count updates if skips arrive before batch cutoff
Driver App Features
Today's route list (sequential stops, optimized order)
Per-stop: customer name, address, ETA, "Navigate" button (opens Google Maps)
QR scanner: scan vendor's QR to confirm pickup (triggers Phase 3 SLA clock)
Stop-by-stop delivery view
Masked in-app call to customer if not home
"Leave at door" option + photo upload
"Failed — Unable to Access" option + photo evidence
Delivery OTP display: show OTP for customer to read aloud / QR for customer to scan
Mark as delivered after OTP confirmation
Customer App Features (During Delivery)
Home screen ETA card updates live: "Driver picked up your food · 3 stops away · ~1:05 PM"
Push notification when driver is 1 stop away
OTP receipt (in-app + SMS)
Delivery confirmed screen → rating prompt
PHASE 8 — Post-Delivery: SLA Engine, Ledger & Ratings
Goal: Automatically calculate penalties, update ledgers, trigger ratings.

SLA Engine (runs after each delivery is confirmed or failed)
Compare timestamps against pre-calculated SLA deadlines
Apply tiered penalties per party:
Vendor penalties (from Vendor Fee):

Delay	Penalty
0–30 min late	0%
30–60 min	15%
60–90 min	30%
90+ min	50%
Driver penalties (from Delivery Fee):

Delay	Penalty
QR scan 0–15 min late	0%
QR scan 15+ min late	2%
Delivery 0–15 min late	0%
15–45 min	15%
45–75 min	30%
75+ min	50%
Write to vendor_ledger and driver_ledger
If delivery failed → refund customer's wallet (full or partial based on fault)
Ratings
24h rating window opens after delivery confirmation
Two-part rating system:
Food/Vendor Rating (1–5 stars + optional text + photo)
Driver Rating (1–5 stars, separate from food)
Gated: only unlocks after customer OTP confirmation
Vendor's and Driver's aggregate ratings recalculated on each new rating
PHASE 9 — Payments, Payouts & Admin
Goal: Money flows in (customer payments) and out (vendor/driver payouts) correctly.

Payments In (Razorpay)
Subscription purchase: full lump sum charged upfront
Premium purchase: one-time payment
Wallet top-up: (post-MVP)
Partial wallet + card: wallet balance auto-deducted, remainder charged to card
Payouts Out (Razorpay Route)
Weekly batch payout to vendors: sum of vendor_ledger net amounts for the week (T+7)
Weekly batch payout to drivers: sum of driver_ledger net amounts for the week (T+7)
Penalty amounts (Vindu's revenue) stay in Vindu's Razorpay account
Admin Panel
Vendor approval / rejection
Delivery zone management
Manual batch job trigger (emergency)
Dispute resolution (view delivery photos, timestamps, override SLA verdicts)
Platform analytics (delivery success rate, average penalty rate, vendor performance)
